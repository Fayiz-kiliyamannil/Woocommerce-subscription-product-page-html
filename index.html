FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which payment gateways can charge renewals automatically?

The official WooCommerce Stripe Gateway and WooCommerce PayPal Payments. Other gateways, such as bank transfer, work with manual renewals: the customer receives an invoice email for each renewal.

Can I give a discount for only the first few months?

Yes. Create a coupon with a recurring discount type and set "Active for payments", for example 3. The discount stops automatically after the third payment.

Can I move my subscriptions from another plugin?

Yes. The import wizard reads export files from WooCommerce Subscriptions, YITH and SUMO, as well as CSV spreadsheets. Each row is checked before import, and renewals are scheduled so billing continues.

What happens when a renewal payment fails?

The subscription goes on hold and the payment is retried on your schedule. The customer is emailed a link to pay. With smart retries, cards that can't succeed, such as stolen or closed cards, aren't retried.

Can customers cancel by themselves?

Yes, from My Account, if you allow it. In Pro you can ask them why with a short survey and show a retention offer first.

Will I lose data when upgrading from the free version?

No. Pro uses the same data as the free plugin, and the free version is deactivated automatically when Pro is activated.

Is it compatible with High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS)?

Yes. The plugin is compatible with WooCommerce HPOS and the classic order storage.

Do subscriptions stop renewing if my license expires?

No. Existing subscriptions keep renewing. An active license is needed for updates, support and the Pro admin features.
